Our Story

A journey through heritage, flavors, and the heart of Puducherry's culinary culture

Heritage Meets Flavor

Nestled in the heart of Heritage Town, Puducherry, Aadeshwar Café stands as a testament to the beautiful fusion of French colonial architecture and authentic Indian street food culture. Our story began with a simple vision: to create a space where the rich culinary traditions of India could be savored within the timeless elegance of Puducherry's French Quarter.

The building that houses our café dates back to the colonial era, with its high ceilings, arched windows, and warm yellow ochre walls that tell stories of a bygone era. We've carefully preserved these architectural gems while infusing the space with the vibrant energy of Indian street food culture.

Every dish we serve carries the legacy of traditional recipes passed down through generations, prepared with the same love and attention to detail that has made Indian street food a global phenomenon.
